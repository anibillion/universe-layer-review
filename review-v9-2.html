The model: claims, not descriptions

Every statement about an entity is a claim. Each claim has a source (who said it), a certainty (established / reported / implied / uncertain / invalid), and a reveal chapter (when a reader is allowed to know it).

The tool never invents a claim. If it does not know when an entity first appeared, it says unknown. If a fact is a hedge, it stays a hedge. If a fact is a claim made by a character, it is recorded as a claim, not as a world fact.

Reveal is per-claim, not per-entity. The Corvid is known from Chapter 41. Sera's connection to the Corvid is held until Chapter 58. Two different things.

You can change any claim's certainty or reveal chapter. You can mark a claim invalid if the tool invented it. You can accept the entity with all claims as shown.
