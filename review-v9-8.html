Three kinds of statement

Claims are statements your text actually makes. Each claim has a source, a certainty, and a canon status. The reader projection shows claims that are canon, established or reported, and past their reveal chapter.

Open questions are things you haven't decided. They are never shown to readers.

Planned reveals are future scenes you intend to write. They are never shown to readers until the scene exists and becomes a claim.

No claim is invented. If the tool does not know something, it says so.
